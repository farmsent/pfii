Danantara Bond Mechanism

The Danantara USD Bond structure provides dual benefits: protecting foreign investors from IDR FX volatility while securing long-term foreign capital for Indonesia's national strategic development. This innovative mechanism enables global investors to access Indonesian growth without currency risk.

Step 1
Bond Purchase
Foreign investor purchases USD-denominated Danantara Bond through authorized channels. Bond terms range from 3-10 years with competitive sovereign yields.
Step 2
Capital Retention
Danantara (Indonesia's sovereign wealth manager) retains USD capital for national strategic infrastructure development including AIDC, energy grids, and transportation.
Step 3
Vault Escrow
Bond asset deposited into secure Vault Escrow system with real-time on-chain attestation. Smart contract governance ensures transparent collateral management.
Step 4
NIDR Minting
Nusantara mints equivalent NIDR stablecoins backed by the bond collateral at prevailing exchange rates. Investor receives NIDR for ecosystem participation.
Step 5
Market Liquidity
NIDR enters secondary market, providing ecosystem-wide liquidity for RWA purchases, trade settlement, and investment activities. Upon bond maturity, investor redeems in original USD.

Reusable ZKP Digital Identity

To eliminate redundant compliance procedures, PFII mandates a single-point Reusable Digital Identity built on W3C Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) standards and Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP). This enables privacy-preserving compliance verification across all ecosystem participants.

One Verification, Universal Access

A single initial KYC/KYB verification generates a cryptographically verifiable credential. Authorized entities—banks, exchanges, tax authorities, trade finance pools—can instantly query identity validity, credit standing, and performance history without exposing raw PII. This dramatically reduces compliance costs while improving regulatory auditability.

  • Billions Network: ZKP identity proofs for institutional verification
  • Privy: Embedded wallet and authentication infrastructure
  • Didit: Decentralized identity verification and credential management

Single-Window Enterprise Interface

PFII's permit concierge service consolidates all regulatory interactions into a unified digital interface:

  • Business Incorporation: Company formation, tax registration, and regulatory licensing
  • Environmental Compliance: AMDAL environmental impact certification
  • Property Services: Land assembly, leasing, and construction permits (PBG/SLF)
  • Regulatory Reporting: Real-time auditability and compliance dashboards
  • Visa Processing: Golden Visa and work permit applications
  • Tax Administration: Automated tax calculation and filing

Legal Framework

PFII operates under Indonesia's Law UU P2SK (Financial Sector Development and Strengthening Law), providing statutory regulatory stability with continuity clauses. Key legal protections include:

  • MIGA Coverage: Political risk insurance structured to meet Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency standards
  • Arbitration Rights: Access to BIDAC, SIAC, and BANI international arbitration
  • Capital Repatriation: Guaranteed 100% foreign capital repatriation rights
  • Regulatory Grandfathering: Existing incentives protected for investment term duration
